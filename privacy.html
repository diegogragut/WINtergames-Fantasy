Privacy

WINter Fantasy is a free fantasy league for the Winter Games 2026 tournament, run by its organizer for friends taking part. Last updated November 10, 2026.

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile photo. We use them only to recognise your account and show your photo in the app.
  • What you enter: your team name, manager name, favourite WINter team and your 7-player squad.

Who can see it

Other signed-in managers see your team name, manager name, favourite team, squad status and points on the league table. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. The site is hosted on Vercel. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else, and there are no ads or trackers.

Deleting your data

Ask the league organizer and they will delete your account, team and squad.
