How WINter Fantasy works

One squad per manager for the whole tournament.

Your squad

  • Pick exactly 7 players: 5 starters and 2 bench.
  • Starters fill GK, DEF, DEF, MID and FWD. Only starters score; the bench earns 0.
  • Maximum 2 players from any one WINter team.
  • Save as often as you like until the first kick-off. Lock early if you want it final.
  • All squads lock at , the first kick-off. Only complete squads score.

Scoring

Goal+5
Assist+2
Match win (every player on the winning team)+1
Goalkeeper clean sheet+3
Yellow card−2
Red card−5

Accounts & saving

  • You sign in with your Claude account. Your squad is saved to it and follows you to any device.
  • Selection % is the share of complete squads that include a player.
  • The league organizer enters each result, scorers, cards and goalkeepers after the final whistle. Points update for everyone at once.
  • Positions are set by the organizer. A player without a position can fill any slot.
