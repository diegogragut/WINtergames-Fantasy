    How WINter Fantasy works

    One team and one squad per manager for the whole tournament.

    Your squad

    • Pick exactly 7 players: 5 starters and 2 bench, with a budget of 100M.
    • Starters: 1 GK, 2 CB and 2 ST. The bench takes any position. Only starters score; the bench earns 0.
    • Captain (C): tap a player's shirt to make him captain. His points count double. You need a captain to save your squad. A captain on the bench scores 0, like any bench player.
    • Prices come from each player's rating: 10 = 25M, 9 = 21M, 8 = 17M, 7 = 13M, 6 = 10M, 5 = 8M, 4 = 6M, 3 = 4M, 2 = 2M.
    • Maximum 2 players from any one WINter team.
    • Save as often as you like until the first kick-off. Use ⇅ on a bench player to swap him with a starter in the same position.
    • All squads lock at , the first kick-off. While squads are locked nobody can change players or captain. Organizers can lock or unlock squads for everyone.
    • Only complete squads score. Each match is scored with your squad exactly as it was at that match's kick-off, so changes made later only count from the next match.

    Scoring

    Goal+5
    Assist+2
    Match win (every player on the winning team)+1
    Goalkeeper clean sheet+3
    Each goal conceded (CBs and goalkeeper)−1
    Yellow card−2
    Red card−5

    The organizer can also add or remove points for a player (for example a man-of-the-match bonus). Each adjustment shows its reason, and players with one show adj next to their points.

    Accounts & saving

    • You sign in with Google. Your team and squad are saved to your account and follow you to any device.
    • After you create your team, an organizer accepts you. Only accepted managers pick squads and appear on the table.
    • Selection % is the share of complete squads that include a player.
    • The league organizer enters each result, scorers, cards and goalkeepers after the final whistle. Points update for everyone at once.
    • Positions are set by the organizer. A player without a position can fill any slot.
